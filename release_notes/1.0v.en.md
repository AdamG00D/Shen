## What's in 1.0

The first release of Shen — an offline OpenPGP key manager for Android.

- **Profiles** (personal, work...), each with its own keys and contacts and an optional own password.
- **Contacts** stay in their profile (the default) or can be shown in all profiles.
- **Keys**: Ed25519 (default), RSA-4096 or v6; change expiry and passphrase, add identities, revoke; a revocation
  certificate is created with every key.
- **Verification** by QR code or by comparing fingerprints, and certification of other people's keys.
- **Encrypt / decrypt / sign / verify** text and files of any size, or encrypt with a password.
- **Encrypted backups** of everything or of one profile, in an open OpenPGP format.
- **Share → Shen** and **Open with** for `.asc` / `.pgp` / `.sig` files.
- Master password + Android Keystore, optional fingerprint unlock bound to the Keystore, auto-lock, screenshots
  blocked, no INTERNET permission.
- Arabic and English, light and dark.
